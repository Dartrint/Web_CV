# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, prospective internship mentors, and collaborators reviewing Trần Thành Đạt's work and academic profile. They need a fast, credible view of his technical depth, research orientation, and product awareness.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual personal CV and portfolio that makes Đạt's path across Mathematics & Computer Science, AI engineering, Business Administration, and software consulting legible through real projects, academic results, and recognition.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents an AI practitioner whose quantitative and mathematical thinking is paired with financial, scientific-research, and management perspectives—not an AI-only profile.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan a personal portfolio before contacting the candidate for AI, quantitative research, software consulting, internship, or collaboration opportunities. They can switch Vietnamese and English, inspect linked GitHub projects, and reach the candidate by email or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site with Vietnamese and English content.
- Preserve all confirmed education, GPA, scholarship, project, GitHub, email, and social-link information.
- Existing project artwork is available in `assets/`.
- The site must remain responsive and accessible on mobile and desktop.

## Brand Commitments

The experience should feel personal and intellectually grounded in mathematical and quantitative thought, AI and technology, scientific research, finance, and management. It must avoid the generic AI-portfolio look.

## Evidence on Hand

- Education, grades, experience, scholarships, and project descriptions are present in `index.html`.
- Project artwork: `assets/healthcare-chatbot.svg`, `assets/jd-agent.svg`, and `assets/tripmate-ai.svg`.
- GitHub project links, email, LinkedIn, and GitHub profile links are present in `index.html`.

## Product Principles

- Let concrete evidence carry credibility.
- Make the interdisciplinary path easy to understand at a glance.
- Treat quantitative reasoning as a lived method, not decorative jargon.
- Keep the path to project proof and contact immediate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide legible contrast, preserve semantic structure and keyboard-accessible controls, and support the Vietnamese and English content equally.
